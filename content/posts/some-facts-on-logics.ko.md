#### fact 1


> Simple DLs (like ALC) can be translated into the FOL class L2 (the FOL fragment
> with no function symbols and only 2 variables).


#### fact 2


> L2 is decidable.


#### fact 3


> FOL with three variables is known to be undecidable.
