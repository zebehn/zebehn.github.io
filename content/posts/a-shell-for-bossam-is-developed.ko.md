A simple shell is devised in Bossam engine. Now, it's possible to
work in the Bossam shell as you work in the JESS shell. The command
repertoire is not rich yet, but you can load ontology or rulebase,
assert rules or facts, ask queries, and initiate inference sessions.


A concise manual is available at [http://mknows.etri.re.kr/wiki/BossamShellManual](http://mknows.etri.re.kr/wiki/BossamShellManual). (*The shell is available in Bossam v0.8. If anybody is interested, please let me know.*)
